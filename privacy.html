Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Mochilabs Video is a ChatGPT plugin created and published by Ika Rifq. This policy explains how information is handled when users interact with the plugin.

1. Information users provide

Mochilabs Video may process content users voluntarily provide in a conversation, such as text prompts, product descriptions, images, illustrations, creative ideas, storyboard instructions, and video preferences.

2. Current data handling

The current version of Mochilabs Video is an instruction-based, skills-only plugin. The developer does not operate a separate plugin database or external API for collecting or storing user conversations, uploaded content, account credentials, or payment information.

User interactions occur within ChatGPT and are also subject to OpenAI's applicable privacy policies, account settings, and service terms.

3. Uploaded images and creative content

Images and other creative materials are used as references to provide the requested creative workflow. Mochilabs Video does not claim ownership of content uploaded by users. Users should only provide materials they own or have permission to use.

4. Data sharing

The current version does not intentionally send user content to a developer-operated API, analytics service, advertising platform, or third-party database, and the developer does not independently sell users' personal information collected through the plugin.

5. Payments

Mochilabs Video does not currently process payments, purchases, or financial transactions inside the plugin.

6. Third-party services

Users may choose to copy generated prompts into third-party AI video, design, marketplace, or editing services. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children's privacy

Mochilabs Video is a general creative productivity tool and is not designed to independently collect personal information from children. Users remain responsible for following ChatGPT's age requirements and applicable laws.

8. Security

Users should not submit passwords, API keys, payment credentials, or other sensitive authentication information in prompts.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if Mochilabs Video adds or changes functionality. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
