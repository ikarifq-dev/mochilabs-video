Mochilabs Video Support

Developer: Ika Rifq

Mochilabs Video helps creators turn product images, illustrations, printable designs, and creative ideas into AI video storyboards and ready-to-use video prompts.

What Mochilabs Video can help with

  • Product video concepts and storyboards
  • TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Etsy, and Shopee video ideas
  • AI video prompts with camera, motion, lighting, and duration
  • UGC, cinematic, product showcase, and cute/kawaii concepts
  • Negative prompts and editing suggestions

Recommended first prompts

“Create a 10-second promotional video storyboard from this image.”

“Create everything from this product image.”

“Create AI video prompts for each scene while preserving the original product design.”

Troubleshooting

If a generated video prompt changes important product details, ask Mochilabs Video to preserve the exact original product design, colors, layout, illustrations, text, proportions, and branding.
